## Homepage photographs (index.html and ar.html, 18 November 2026)

The homepage is built from a small set of high-resolution Wikimedia Commons photographs (`assets/fotos/`), all CC BY / CC BY-SA. The opening scroll film (`assets/frames/f_film_*`) starts from Daniel Case's photograph of an acacia at sunset in the Maasai Mara; its motion (clouds, grass, sun, distant birds) was generated from that photograph with Google Veo 3.1 (8 s, 1080p, locked-off camera, source clip in `source/film/opening-v1.mp4`), the push-in is applied in the frame render, and the canopy is a separate layer cut from the photograph. Nine chapter films (`assets/capitulos/`: `f_caravan_*`, `f_lion_*`, `f_vehicle_*`, `f_elephants_*`, `f_camp_*`, `f_crater_*`, `f_balloon_*`, `f_sunrise_*`, `f_nungwi_*`, 64 frames each) were generated the same way from the caravan, lion, vehicle-open, elephants-track, camp-deck, crater-light, balloon-aerial, sunrise and nungwi photographs below (Veo 3.1, 8 s, locked camera; clips in `source/film/clip-*.mp4`). The stock video clips used briefly on 18 November were removed the same day. The full table with photographer names and source pages is on `credits.html` and in `assets/fotos/credits.json`.
