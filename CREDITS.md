## Homepage footage and photographs (index.html and ar.html, 18 November 2026)

The homepage was rebuilt on real footage after client feedback. The opening film (`assets/frames/`) and the 22 chapter loops in `assets/clips/` are Pexels videos (Pexels licence: free for commercial use, no attribution required). The real airstrip, camp, lodge, balloon, Kilimanjaro and Stone Town photographs in `assets/photos/` are Wikimedia Commons files under CC BY / CC BY-SA; the full table with photographer names and source pages is on `credits.html` and in `assets/photos/credits.json`. The journey card images are stills from the same clips and photographs. No AI-generated media remains on the homepage; the earlier generated set was removed on 18 November 2026.
