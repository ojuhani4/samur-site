Footage and photographs

Every moving image on the Samur homepage is real footage of East Africa, and every still is a real photograph of a real airstrip, camp, lodge or street. Nothing is generated. The clips are licensed from Pexels; the photographs are from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licences that ask for the attribution given here.
