Photographs

Every image on the Samur homepage is a real photograph licensed from Wikimedia Commons under the Creative Commons licences below, which ask for this attribution. Most were taken in Tanzania. The opening and the contact background come from the Maasai Mara in Kenya, and the open safari vehicle was photographed in Olifants West, South Africa; they stand for the kind of light and vehicle you will find, not for the exact place. No still image is generated. The opening film starts from Daniel Case's photograph of an acacia at sunset: the drifting clouds, moving grass and sinking sun were animated from that photograph with an image-to-video model (Google Veo 3.1, 8 seconds), the camera push is added in the frame render, and the canopy is a separate layer cut from the same photograph. Nine chapters (the Caravan, the lion, the open vehicle, the elephants, the camp, the crater light, the balloon, the sunrise and Nungwi) are animated the same way from the photographs listed below, with a locked camera: wind, light, water, sky and the animals themselves move; nothing is added that the photograph did not contain.
